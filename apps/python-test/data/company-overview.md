# FlowAI - Company Overview

FlowAI is a next-generation revenue intelligence platform that helps B2B SaaS companies automate their sales processes, manage leads, and optimize revenue operations through AI-powered analytics and workflow automation.

## Mission
Empower every revenue team with AI-driven insights to close deals faster and predict revenue with confidence.

## Founded
2021 | Series B ($45M raised) | 500+ customers | 200 employees

## Key Products
- **FlowAI Sales** - AI-powered sales engagement platform with predictive lead scoring, automated outreach sequences, and real-time pipeline analytics
- **FlowAI CRM** - Modern CRM with native AI capabilities, automated data enrichment, and intelligent relationship mapping
- **FlowAI Revenue Intelligence** - Conversation intelligence, deal risk scoring, and revenue forecasting powered by machine learning
