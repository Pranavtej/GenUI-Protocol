# FlowAI CRM - Data & Analytics

## Overview
FlowAI CRM is a modern, AI-native customer relationship management platform that automates data entry, enriches records, and provides actionable intelligence across the customer lifecycle.

## Customer Data Management
- **Unified Customer Profile**: Single view of customer with 360-degree data model
  - Contact information and communication history
  - Account hierarchy and relationship mapping
  - Deal history and opportunity tracking
  - Support tickets and product usage data
  - Contract terms and renewal dates
- **Automated Data Enrichment**: AI-powered enrichment from 50+ data sources
  - Company firmographics (industry, revenue, employee count)
  - Technographic data (tools and technologies used)
  - Social profiles and news mentions
  - Funding and IPO events
- **Data Quality Engine**
  - Deduplication with fuzzy matching
  - Standardization of phone, address, and company names
  - Missing field identification and auto-fill suggestions
  - Data freshness scoring and stale record alerts
