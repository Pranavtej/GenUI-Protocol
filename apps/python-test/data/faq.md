# FlowAI - Frequently Asked Questions

## General FAQs

**Q: How does FlowAI integrate with my existing tools?**
A: FlowAI offers native integrations with 200+ tools including Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, SalesLoft, Slack, and Microsoft Teams. We also provide a REST API and webhooks for custom integrations. Most integrations can be set up in under 10 minutes.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. FlowAI is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We offer SSO/SAML, role-based access control, and audit logging. Enterprise customers can request on-premise or VPC deployment.

**Q: How long does implementation take?**
A: Most customers are up and running within 2-4 weeks. Our customer success team provides dedicated onboarding, and we offer data migration services for customers moving from other platforms.

**Q: Do you offer a free trial?**
A: Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial of our Growth plan with no credit card required. Enterprise prospects get a personalized demo and a 30-day proof of concept.

## Sales & CRM FAQs

**Q: How does FlowAI lead scoring work?**
A: Our AI models analyze historical conversion data to identify patterns that predict deal success. Scores are calculated based on demographic fit (35%), behavioral engagement (40%), and intent signals (25%). Scores update in real-time and range from 0-100.

**Q: What is lead scoring accuracy?**
A: Our predictive models achieve 89% accuracy in identifying leads that will convert within 90 days, and our top-quartile leads are 5.2x more likely to convert than bottom-quartile leads.

**Q: Can I customize pipeline stages?**
A: Yes. Pipeline stages are fully customizable with drag-and-drop workflow builder. You can define stage names, probabilities, required fields, and automated actions on stage transitions.

## Technical FAQs

**Q: What is the API rate limit?**
A: Standard API rate limit is 1,000 requests per minute per organization. Enterprise plans can request higher limits. Our GraphQL API supports batched queries for efficient data retrieval.

**Q: Do you support real-time data sync?**
A: Yes. We support real-time webhook events for lead creation, stage changes, deal updates, and more. Our platform also supports CDC (Change Data Capture) for enterprise data warehouse sync.

**Q: What uptime SLA do you offer?**
A: Growth plan: 99.9% uptime SLA. Enterprise plan: 99.99% uptime SLA with financial guarantees. We publish real-time status at status.flowai.com.

## Support FAQs

**Q: What support options are available?**
A: Starter: Email support with 48-hour response. Growth: Priority email and chat support with 24-hour response. Enterprise: 24/7 phone, email, and chat support with dedicated CSM. All plans have access to our knowledge base and community forum.

**Q: How do I export my data?**
A: You can export all data via the UI (CSV/Excel) or our API (JSON/CSV). Enterprise plans include automated data exports to Snowflake, BigQuery, and S3.
