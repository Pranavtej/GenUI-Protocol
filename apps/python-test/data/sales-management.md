# FlowAI Sales Management

## Overview
FlowAI Sales is an AI-powered sales engagement platform that helps sales teams automate outreach, prioritize leads, and close deals faster. It integrates seamlessly with your existing tech stack and provides real-time intelligence across the entire sales cycle.

## Key Features

### Pipeline Management
- Visual pipeline builder with drag-and-drop deal staging
- Custom deal stages with automated progression rules
- Real-time pipeline analytics and velocity tracking
- Weighted pipeline forecasting with AI confidence scoring
- Historical pipeline comparison and trend analysis

### Sales Engagement
- Multi-channel outreach sequences (email, phone, LinkedIn, SMS)
- AI-powered send time optimization
- Automated follow-up scheduling and task creation
- A/B testing for email subject lines and content
- Template library with dynamic personalization variables
- Reply detection and smart thread management

### Deal Management
- Deal scoring based on fit, engagement, and intent signals
- Automated deal stage progression based on trigger events
- Deal room collaboration with customer-facing portals
- Competitive tracking and win/loss analysis
- Contract lifecycle management with e-signature integration

### Forecasting
- AI-powered revenue forecasting with confidence intervals
- Bottom-up and top-down forecasting models
- Scenario planning and what-if analysis
- Real-time forecast vs. actuals tracking
- Rep-level and team-level forecast rollups
- Historical forecast accuracy scoring

## Integrations
- HubSpot, Salesforce, Outreach, SalesLoft
- Slack, Microsoft Teams
- ZoomInfo, Clearbit, Lusha
- DocuSign, PandaDoc
- Snowflake, BigQuery

## Analytics & Reporting
- Real-time sales activity dashboard
- Conversion funnel analysis with segment filtering
- Sales velocity metrics by rep, team, and segment
- Activity effectiveness scoring and coaching insights
- Custom report builder with drag-and-drop interface
- Scheduled report delivery via email and Slack
