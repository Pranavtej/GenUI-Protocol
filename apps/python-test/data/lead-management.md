# FlowAI Lead Management

## Overview
FlowAI Lead Management provides end-to-end lead tracking and nurturing capabilities powered by AI. From first touch to closed won, our platform ensures no lead falls through the cracks.

## Lead Scoring
- **Predictive Lead Scoring**: ML models trained on historical conversion data score leads from 0-100 based on:
  - Demographic fit (industry, company size, role, location)
  - Behavioral signals (email opens, site visits, content downloads)
  - Engagement patterns (meeting attendance, product demo requests)
  - Intent data (purchase intent keywords, competitor research)
- **Real-time Scoring Updates**: Scores update dynamically as new data arrives
- **Custom Scoring Models**: Train custom models on your closed-won data
- **Score Decay**: Leads with no activity automatically decay over configurable periods

## Lead Routing
- Round-robin assignment with load balancing
- Territory-based routing with zip code to rep mapping
- Skill-based routing (industry expertise, deal size)
- Lead rotation and recycle rules
- Booth lead assignment with QR code scanning
- Slack alerts for hot lead assignment

## Lead Nurturing
- Automated drip campaigns based on lead score segments
- Behavior-triggered email sequences
- Multi-channel nurturing (email, LinkedIn ads, retargeting)
- Content recommendation engine
- Lead re-engagement campaigns for stale leads
- Meeting scheduling with calendar integration

## Lead Qualification
- BANT and MEDDIC framework support
- AI-powered lead qualification scoring
- Questionnaire-based lead grading
- Qualification criteria automation
- Lead status tracking (New, Contacted, Qualified, Unqualified, Lost)

## Lead Source Tracking
- UTM parameter capture and attribution
- Campaign source tracking (Google Ads, LinkedIn, Organic, Referral)
- Event and webinar lead capture
- API-based lead ingestion from partner platforms
- Channel performance analytics by source
