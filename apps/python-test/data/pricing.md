# FlowAI Pricing Plans

## Pricing Tiers

### Starter - $79/month per user
- Up to 5 users
- Basic CRM and lead management
- Email sequencing (500 emails/month/user)
- Standard pipeline management
- Basic analytics dashboard
- Email support (48h response)

### Growth - $149/month per user
- Up to 25 users
- Full CRM with automation
- AI-powered lead scoring
- Multi-channel outreach (email + LinkedIn)
- Custom pipeline stages and workflows
- Advanced analytics and forecasting
- Priority support (24h response)
- API access

### Enterprise - Custom pricing
- Unlimited users
- Dedicated AI model training
- Custom integrations and SSO
- Advanced security and compliance (SOC 2, GDPR)
- Dedicated customer success manager
- Custom reporting and data warehouse sync
- 99.99% SLA
- 24/7 phone and chat support
- On-premise deployment option

## Add-ons
- **Revenue Intelligence**: $50/user/month - Conversation intelligence, deal risk scoring
- **Data Enrichment**: $0.05/record - Automated company and contact enrichment
- **Advanced Forecasting**: $30/user/month - AI-powered forecasting with scenario modeling
- **API Credits**: $0.001/call - Additional API calls beyond plan limits

## Discounts
- Annual billing: 2 months free (17% discount)
- Non-profit: 50% discount
- Startup program: Free for first year (under $1M ARR, less than 20 employees)

## Recent Changes (May 2026)
- Starter plan introduced (previously only Growth and Enterprise)
- Growth plan price increased from $129 to $149/user/month
- Revenue Intelligence moved from Enterprise-only to add-on for all plans
- New Data Enrichment add-on launched
