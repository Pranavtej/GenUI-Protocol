# Mascot options

Four original concepts live in [`options/`](options). They are generated by `scripts/mascot/opt_*.py` (Pillow only, no assets): edit colours or shapes and re-run `python3 scripts/mascot/opt_morph.py` (etc.). Open [`options/preview.html`](options/preview.html) to see them on light and dark backgrounds.

## Prompts for generating a variant in an image/animation tool

Shared style line, prepend to any of them:

> Pixel art, 64×64 logical pixel grid, no anti-aliasing, crisp hard 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1f3a45), flat limited palette (teal #2a9d8f, blue #2563eb, orange #f4845f, green #16a34a, white, greys), transparent background, seamless looping animation, retro 16-bit sprite style, centred with margin, no text, no drop shadow other than a flat grey ellipse.

- **A — Curly:** a round smiling teal face with pink cheeks, held between a pair of blue curly braces `{ }` (a JSON smiley). The braces gently breathe in and out. Every few frames a tiny UI widget (browser card, bar chart, button) pops out above its head with an overshoot bounce and yellow sparkles, then shrinks away; the eyes look up at it; one blink per loop.
- **B — Morph:** a glossy teal jelly blob with a tiny smile and white square eyes that squashes and stretches, then morphs into a UI card (blue title bar, grey tile, text lines) with the same eyes on the title bar, back to blob, into a blue pill button, back to blob, into a white checkbox that draws a green check mark. Hops slightly during each morph, flat grey ground shadow.
- **C — Builder:** a small blue-bodied robot with a teal head, dark visor with two cyan eyes and an orange antenna ball, standing on the left. UI blocks (a bar-chart panel, a KPI card, a blue button) fall from above one after another and stack into a dashboard on a grey platform; the robot raises an arm as each block drops, then jumps with both arms up and yellow sparkles when the dashboard is complete; blocks pop away in a burst and the loop restarts.
- **D — Sprout:** a teal tree-spirit (a round body with a small green sprout on its head, white eyes, pink cheeks) at the bottom. Thin branches grow from it to two light-teal round nodes, then to four small UI widget icons (card, chart, button, toggle) that pop in with bounce; one widget flashes yellow and swaps (a patch); everything folds away and regrows. It is an AST that grows into a UI.

Tip: ask the tool for a sprite sheet of the frames in one row, then assemble the GIF yourself so timing, looping and transparency stay under your control, e.g. `ffmpeg -framerate 11 -i frame_%d.png -vf "split[a][b];[a]palettegen=reserve_transparent=1[p];[b][p]paletteuse" out.gif`. Image models asked for an "animated GIF" directly usually return blurry, off-grid pixels.
