# Mascot: regenerating or restyling

`genui-mascot.gif` (512px) and `genui-mascot-256.gif` are generated by `python3 scripts/mascot/make_mascot.py` (Pillow only, no assets). Edit the palette or shapes in that script and re-run.

To make a different version with an image/animation model, a prompt that matches this style:

> Pixel art animated mascot, 64×64 logical pixel grid, no anti-aliasing, hard 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1f3a45), limited flat palette of 8–10 colours. A small teal bird (#2a9d8f body, darker teal back wing, white belly, orange beak) wearing a tiny dark-blue necktie, flying to the right like a courier. It carries a small browser-window card by two strings from its feet: white card with a blue title bar, a grey tile, grey text lines and a small green bar. Seamless 8-frame loop at ~11 fps: wings flap through a full cycle, body bobs one pixel up and down, the card swings gently and lags behind, one blink, a few pale speed lines behind the tail. Transparent background, side view, centred with margin, 1:1 canvas, crisp pixels, retro 16-bit sprite style, no text, no shadow.

Tips: generate a sprite sheet of the 8 frames in a row, then assemble the GIF yourself (e.g. `ffmpeg -framerate 11 -i frame_%d.png -vf "split[a][b];[a]palettegen=reserve_transparent=1[p];[b][p]paletteuse" out.gif`) so frame timing, looping and transparency are under your control; "animated GIF" output from image models is usually blurry and off-grid.
